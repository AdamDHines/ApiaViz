Reading the replay. All three use wiring seed 19 and the same learning and navigation settings. Each video contains the full trial. The first two steps are slowed threefold; the remainder plays at one decision per second. This is an explanatory playback pace, not a measured walking speed. The videos are silent and include on-screen explanations.

Detailed view versus model input. The large panorama is rendered separately for viewing. It never enters the model. The small input panel, feature maps, firing cells and heading scores come from the original low-resolution computation. During movement, the sensor panels hold the last evaluated view. The map is also for the viewer; it does not guide the agent.

Why include a difficult run? These examples show different behaviours, including a failure, rather than suggesting that every route succeeds. The conclusions about performance come from the full evaluation, not these three clips.
